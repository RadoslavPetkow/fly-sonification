The melody is not the fly's. Each voice is one anatomical family of descending neurons, and its pitch was assigned by name order before any audio was played. What the fly supplies is the timing.

Sound reaches these neurons across a single synapse; two synapses in, the signal is at chance. Pentatonic mode quantises the same spikes to a scale — it sounds better and proves less, which is why the analysis used the chromatic version.
